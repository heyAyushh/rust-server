😺 Purr-fect Companions

Cats have been domesticated for over 10,000 years and have become one of the most beloved pets worldwide. Their independent yet affectionate nature makes them ideal companions for people of all ages.

😸 Natural Hunters

With razor-sharp reflexes and incredible agility, cats are born hunters. Their retractable claws, excellent night vision, and sensitive whiskers make them formidable predators—even if they're just hunting a toy mouse!

😻 Masters of Sleep

Cats sleep an average of 12-16 hours per day! They're crepuscular creatures, meaning they're most active during dawn and dusk. Those power naps help them conserve energy for important activities like zooming around at 3 AM.

🎉 Fun Cat Facts

  • Cats can rotate their ears 180 degrees
  • A group of cats is called a "clowder"
  • Cats spend 30-50% of their day grooming
  • Each cat's nose print is unique, like a fingerprint
  • Cats can jump up to 6 times their length
  • The oldest cat ever lived to be 38 years old
  • Cats have over 20 vocalizations, including the meow
  • A cat's purr vibrates at 25-150 Hz, which can promote healing
